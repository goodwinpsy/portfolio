Grounding

5–4–3–2–1 Grounding

A simple sensory grounding exercise. Move through each step slowly and notice what is around you.

5

things you can see

Look around slowly. Name five things you can see.

4

things you can feel

Notice four things you can physically feel or touch.

3

things you can hear

Pause and identify three sounds you can hear.

2

things you can smell

Notice two smells around you, if available.

1

thing you can taste

Notice one taste in your mouth, or imagine a familiar neutral taste.

These resources are for general educational and self-practice purposes. They are not a substitute for individual psychological assessment, diagnosis or treatment. If an exercise increases distress, stop and seek appropriate professional support.

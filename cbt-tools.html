CBT Tools & Resources

Practical tools for reflection.

Guided exercises designed to help you observe connections between situations, thoughts, emotions and behaviours.

These resources are intended for education and self-reflection. They are not a substitute for individual psychological assessment, diagnosis or psychotherapy.

🧠 Thought Record

Notice an automatic thought and work towards a more balanced response.

🔄 CBT Cycle

Map a situation, thought, emotion, behaviour and outcome.

⚖️ Evidence Check

Explore evidence supporting and challenging a distressing thought.

🌱 Activity & Mood

Reflect on activities, engagement and changes in mood.

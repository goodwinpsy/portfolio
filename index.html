5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding Technique

Use your 5 senses to calm your nervous system and bring your mind back to the present moment.

5
LOOK: 5 THINGS YOU CAN SEE

Look around and quietly name 5 objects around you (e.g., pen, clock, doorway).

4
TOUCH: 4 THINGS YOU CAN FEEL

Notice 4 physical sensations (e.g., feet on the floor, clothes on shoulders, cool desk).

3
LISTEN: 3 THINGS YOU CAN HEAR

Listen for 3 background sounds (e.g., hum of a fan, distant birds, your breath).

2
SMELL: 2 THINGS YOU CAN SMELL

Notice 2 scents around you, or take two deep, deliberate breaths.

1
TASTE: 1 THING YOU CAN TASTE

Focus on 1 taste in your mouth, or take a refreshing sip of water.

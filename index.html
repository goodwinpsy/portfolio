FAQ

Questions you may have before booking.

What happens during the first consultation?

The first consultation focuses on understanding your concerns, relevant background, current difficulties and goals, and deciding what kind of support may be appropriate.

Are online consultations available?

Yes. Online consultations are available in addition to in-person consultations, subject to suitability and applicable professional requirements.

Where are in-person consultations available?

In-person consultations are available at ReSolve Counselling & Psychotherapy and Nirmala Medical Centre in Muvattupuzha.

How do I schedule a consultation?

Use the Schedule Consultation button to access the booking calendar, or contact the practice through WhatsApp or phone.

Will I need medication?

Psychologists do not prescribe medication. Where medication evaluation is clinically indicated, appropriate psychiatric consultation may be recommended.

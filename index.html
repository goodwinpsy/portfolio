5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding Technique

Calm your nervous system and bring your mind back to the present moment during high anxiety.

5
LOOK: 5 THINGS YOU CAN SEE

Quietly observe and name 5 objects around you (e.g., pen, clock, doorway).

4
TOUCH: 4 THINGS YOU CAN FEEL

Focus on 4 physical sensations (e.g., feet on the floor, shirt texture, cool desk).

3
LISTEN: 3 THINGS YOU CAN HEAR

Listen closely for 3 background sounds (e.g., fan hum, distant cars, breathing).

2
SMELL: 2 THINGS YOU CAN SMELL

Notice 2 scents around you, or take two slow, deep breaths.

1
TASTE: 1 THING YOU CAN TASTE

Focus on 1 taste in your mouth, or take a refreshing sip of water.
